BUS D

Signal Path · FAQ

ISOLATION · PRIVACY · SAFETY

Does it read #general or other text channels?

Never. Strict voice-channel isolation: it only reads messages typed inside the built-in text chat of the voice channel it is connected to.

What happens when everyone leaves?

It is audience-aware. Speech pauses the moment no humans remain, and the bot disconnects cleanly after a 60-second grace period.

Can someone inject "ignore previous instructions"?

No. Every generation's transcript is verified against narration rules — text is read strictly verbatim, never answered, never conversed with.

Why isn't it reading my message?

Check three things: you ran /reader join while connected, you are typing in that exact VC's chat panel, and you are not rate-limited for a burst of messages.
